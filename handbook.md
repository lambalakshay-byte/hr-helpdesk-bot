# Nimbus Technologies India Pvt. Ltd. — Employee Handbook (SAMPLE / FICTIONAL)

Version 4.2 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: People & Culture (HR)

## Section 1. About this handbook
1.1 This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Nimbus Technologies. Contractors and interns are covered by their own agreements.
1.2 Where this handbook and an individual employment contract differ, the contract applies.
1.3 HR may update this handbook; the latest version is always on the HR portal.

## Section 2. Working hours and attendance
2.1 Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour break.
2.2 Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Employees should be available during these hours.
2.3 Three late arrivals (after 10:00 AM without prior approval) in a calendar month result in a deduction of 0.5 day of casual leave.
2.4 Attendance is recorded through the HR portal. Missed check-ins must be regularised within 3 working days.

## Section 3. Leave policy
3.1 Annual leave: Full-year entitlement is 18 days per calendar year. New joiners receive a pro-rated entitlement (see 3.8).
3.2 Applying for annual leave: Leave of 3 days or more must be applied for at least 5 working days in advance. Shorter leave needs at least 1 working day notice, except in emergencies. All leave needs manager approval on the HR portal.
3.3 Carry-forward: A maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave can be carried forward to the next year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse. Sick leave and casual leave cannot be carried forward.
3.4 Encashment: Annual leave cannot be encashed during employment. At exit, unused annual leave (including carry-forward) is encashed at basic pay divided by 26 per day.
3.5 Sick leave: 12 days per calendar year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days. Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness only.
3.6 Casual leave: 6 days per calendar year. A maximum of 2 consecutive days can be taken as casual leave at a time.
3.7 Special leave:
 - Maternity leave: 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children.
 - Paternity leave: 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth.
 - Bereavement leave: 5 working days for the death of an immediate family member.
 - Marriage leave: 3 working days, once during employment.
3.8 New joiners: Annual leave is credited at 1.5 days per full month remaining in the calendar year from the joining month. Sick and casual leave are pro-rated by the same ratio (months remaining divided by 12), rounded to the nearest whole day.
3.9 Leave without pay (LWP) may be approved by the manager and HR for up to 15 days in a calendar year, and requires at least 5 working days' notice.
3.10 Public holidays that fall within a leave period are not counted as leave days.

## Section 4. Company holidays (2026)
4.1 The company observes 10 holidays: 26 January (Republic Day), 4 March (Holi), 15 August (Independence Day), 2 October (Gandhi Jayanti), 20 October (Dussehra), 8 November (Diwali), 9 November (Diwali holiday), 25 December (Christmas), 1 May (Labour Day), 14 April (Ambedkar Jayanti).
4.2 Where a holiday falls on a weekend, no compensatory day is given.
4.3 Each employee may also choose 2 optional holidays per year from a list published on the HR portal, with 7 days' notice.

## Section 5. Hybrid work and work from home
5.1 Employees work from the office at least 3 days a week and may work from home up to 2 days a week, as agreed with their manager.
5.2 New joiners work from the office full-time for their first 2 weeks.
5.3 Working from home from a different city (work-from-anywhere) is allowed up to 10 working days per calendar year with manager and HR approval.
5.4 A monthly internet reimbursement of Rs 1,000 is paid to employees on the hybrid arrangement, against a submitted bill.

## Section 6. Payroll and reimbursements
6.1 Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available on the HR portal by the 3rd of the following month.
6.2 Reimbursement claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of the expense. Claims after 30 days are not accepted.
6.3 Tax-saving investment declarations are collected in April and verified in December. Employees who do not submit proofs are taxed without those deductions.
6.4 Salary-related queries about an individual's pay (for example, breakdown or revision) are handled by HR and Payroll directly and are not answered through the helpdesk bot.

## Section 7. Benefits
7.1 Group health insurance: Rs 5,00,000 family floater covering the employee, spouse, and up to 2 children, from the date of joining. Parents can be added as a top-up at the employee's own cost.
7.2 Group accident cover: Rs 10,00,000 for all employees.
7.3 Employee Assistance Programme (EAP): up to 6 free confidential counselling sessions per calendar year, via the EAP helpline 1800-000-0000 (sample number).
7.4 Learning budget: Rs 20,000 per employee per financial year for approved courses and certifications, claimed after completion with a certificate.

## Section 8. Probation and performance
8.1 The probation period is 6 months for all new joiners. A confirmation letter is issued after a satisfactory review.
8.2 Performance reviews take place twice a year, in March and September. Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5.
8.3 The annual promotion and increment cycle is run in April.

## Section 9. Code of conduct and workplace safety
9.1 Nimbus has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying.
9.2 Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee (POSH). Contact: posh@nimbus-example.com. All complaints are handled confidentially.
9.3 Employees can report other ethics concerns confidentially to ethics@nimbus-example.com. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is prohibited.
9.4 Complaints and sensitive matters should not be handled through this chatbot. The chatbot can only direct employees to the right contact and raise a confidential ticket.

## Section 10. Resignation and exit
10.1 The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees and 30 days during probation.
10.2 A notice-period buy-out is possible with written approval from the manager and HR.
10.3 Exit clearance (IT assets, access cards, finance dues) must be completed on the last working day.
10.4 Full and final settlement is completed within 45 days of the last working day.

## Section 11. IT and assets
11.1 Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.
11.2 A lost or stolen company device must be reported to IT and HR within 24 hours.
11.3 Company data must not be stored on personal cloud accounts or personal devices.

## Section 12. HR contacts and service levels
12.1 HR Helpdesk: hr-helpdesk@nimbus-example.com · Extension 1100 · Monday to Friday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
12.2 HR responds to tickets within 2 working days. Tickets marked sensitive or confidential are reviewed within 1 working day.
12.3 Matters not covered in this handbook (for example, relocation support, sabbaticals, or equity/stock-option plans) must be raised with HR as a ticket.
